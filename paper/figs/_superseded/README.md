# Orphan figure files (not read by any figure of the draft)

Moved here 2026-09-26; nothing was deleted. No `\input`/`table` in `paper/main.tex` or `paper/figs/*.tex`
reads these files.

- `falsifier_spectrum.dat`, `falsifier_sweep.dat` — data of the removed `fig_falsifier`; generator `src/_superseded/export_falsifier_dat.py`.
- `gauss.dat` — the bit-flip sweep of `⟨ΣG²⟩` (the content of `data/2026-08-18_gausslaw_falsifier.json`); `fig_gausslaw` plots `gauss_state.dat` instead, whose generator is not yet committed (known gap).
- `gauss_spectral.dat` — output of a circular Gauss-law "spectral screen" (clean-state moments against a reconstruction of the contaminated state) that the 2026-08-26 integrity pass dropped; its generator is not in this repository.
- `heron_sweep.dat` — a weight-misplacement sweep on the companion's Heron reconstruction (`f`, `r0`, `r1`), i.e. the reconstruction-vs-reconstruction lineage of `src/_superseded/run_heron_screen.py`; no committed generator; `fig_heron` does not read it.
- `sep_exemplar.dat` — an exemplar point set for `fig_separating` that the figure does not read.
- `fig_circuit.tex` — an earlier native-TikZ circuit; the draft includes `fig_circuit_qtk.pdf`, whose source is `paper/figs/src/fig_circuit_qtk.tex`.
- `sep_2026-08-31/` — the previous `fig_separating` data (`sep_clean`, `sep_krylov`, `sep_trunc`, `sep_ac_other`, `sep_ac_sep`.dat; x, y rounded to 4 significant figures and floored at 0.03) and the figure source as of 2026-09-26. Superseded 2026-09-27 by the files `src/separating_counts.py` now writes from the sealed record (full precision, no floor, id column; the 24 in-region spurious points are split into 20 weight-preserving + 4 weight-changing, and the two non-spurious in-region points, ids 162 and 184, are drawn from `sep_region_nonac.dat`). The old values agree with the new recount to rounding.
- `christoffel_maxW_grid400.dat` — the previous `christoffel_maxW.dat` (read by `fig_christoffel` (b) and the `fig_bracketing` inset): max_t W_n on a 400-point grid (0.999843 / 0.964330 / 0.885610 / 0.875125). Superseded 2026-09-27 (plan R9) by the exact maxima from root-finding that `src/export_christoffel_dat.py` now writes (see `data/2026-09-27_theory_numerics.json`, key `R9_christoffel_max_Wn`).
- `christoffel_poles_with_zero_weight_rows.dat` — the previous `christoffel_poles.dat` (not read by any figure); its last three rows were zero-weight Fock atoms (residue ~1e-27) whose order depended on round-off. Since 2026-09-27 `src/export_christoffel_dat.py` lists only atoms with weight > 1e-12.
- `fig_device_2026-09-02.tex` — the previous `fig_device` source: a single panel with the device points and ONE noiseless Aer seed (seed 1, `data/aer_noiseless_baseline.json`) typed in as inline coordinates. Superseded 2026-09-27 (plan R1/R12, items B2/B3): the Aer seed-1 values sit at the 19.5/17.7/81.4/98.3th percentiles of 2000 noiseless replicas, so a single seed misrepresents the comparison. `fig_device` now reads `device_points.dat`, `device_band.dat` (noiseless mean ± 1 sd) and `device_uniform.dat` (uniform in-sector sampler), written by `src/export_device_dat.py` from `data/2026-09-27_delta0_reference_mc.json` (`src/delta0_reference_mc.py`).
